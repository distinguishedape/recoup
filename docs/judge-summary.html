Realand where the line is drawn

What runs against live Razorpay, and what does not

Real — live API

  • Razorpay signed a payment.failed event and delivered it over the public internet; the receiver verified the signature, classified it and audited it.
  • Detection found ₹3,497 at risk across three abandoned checkouts on its first run against the live account.
  • Payment links are created for real, through the live API, and appear in the dashboard.
  • Classification measured at 99.4% against 2,000 known-truth events.

Simulated — and why

  • Whether a contacted customer pays is modelled, not observed.
  • Razorpay test mode cannot inject a decline reason. All eight documented error-scenario cards were paid; all eight returned one indistinguishable string.
  • So every recovery probability is a declared assumption, swept across three bands.
  • The live rail's charge() raises rather than returning a plausible number — a simulated outcome cannot enter through the path labelled real.

Dead cards are where the thesis lives: ₹11,996 against ₹2,56,874, twenty-onefold, because Recoup asks for a new card instead of hammering one that cannot work.

It loses ₹30,490 on TRANSIENT_ISSUER. An outage is the one cause a plain retry ladder already suits, and Recoup spends fewer attempts on it by design. It gives up ₹499 on RISK_DECLINE on purpose, because that routes to a human rather than arguing with a bank's fraud decision. Neither is a bug; both are the cost of a policy stated in advance.

Containedthe model proposes, policy disposes

Where the AI sits, and what holds it

A lookup table resolves 28 of Razorpay's documented decline reasons — free, instant, and incapable of hallucinating. Only the reasons Razorpay itself documents as not disclosing the cause reach the model.

Identical evidence produces identical prompts and the cache collapses them, which is why 2,000 subjects cost six calls. Everything the model returns is validated against closed enums and a template allowlist before anything acts on it: asked to invent a failure class, retry a dead card five times, or write its own threatening copy, every proposal is rejected and the deterministic planner runs instead.

Its clearest earned contribution is dead cards hiding behind uninformative reason strings — positive in all twelve cells. Its effect on the total is a mean one cohort contradicts, and is reported as not replicating rather than as a number that happens to be positive.

Refusedsix rules, each naming itself

Consent is not a retry policy

MANDATE_REVOKED → stop. Never charges, never messages.

Six rules gate every action and each names itself when it denies: contact window (08:00–19:00 IST), template allowlist, contact rate limit, opt-out stop, promise-to-pay suppression, per-cause attempt budget. A blocked contact returns to the clock at the next permitted hour rather than being discarded — turning a rule about when into a rule about whether is a far more expensive policy than anyone agreed to, wearing a compliance costume.

The executor accepts exactly one type, and that type is produced only by the policy gate. Direct construction raises, and so does dataclasses.replace — the route that would have let someone forge permission by accident while adjusting a scheduled time.

Auditedon our own evidence

We tried to break our own result

The experiment ran four more times across independent cohorts to see what survived. Then the measurement pipeline itself was audited, and four defects were found and published — a run where the model was silently disconnected, an audit log that stacked runs on top of each other, a reproduce-offline claim that had quietly stopped working, and a configuration freeze that was hiding prompt changes from the numbers it certified.

Every serious defect we found was in the measurement, not the product.

All four were in the harness rather than the pipeline, which is the harder place to look and the place a reader has least reason to trust. The pattern they share is that each claim was true when written and stopped being true without anything failing — so the fix in every case was to make the claim executable: a recorded fixture instead of a comment, a runner that refuses to publish a degraded bundle, a freeze that registers the prompts.

The habit is older than the defects. The second commit in this repository, before the domain models and before any recovery logic, was the engineering decision log — now 65 entries of what was chosen and what it costs if the choice was wrong.

Askedwhat we need that does not exist

One API away from ending the simulation

Razorpay exposes no manual-retry API for subscription invoices, and does not support manual charging of a domestic card.

That single gap is why recovery outcomes are modelled instead of measured. Everything else in this pipeline — ingestion, detection, classification, link creation, the audit trail — runs live today.
