What a failed auto-debit is worth once you read the reason first.

Recoup classifies the decline cause, picks the intervention that matches it, and executes inside hard compliance limits. Every figure below is read from evidence/sweep.json, evidence/schedule-ablation.md and pytest --collect-only — not typed in by hand.

Read this as a model, not a bank statement. The pipeline is real — Razorpay signs the webhook and the payment links are live. Whether a contacted customer pays is simulated, because Razorpay's sandbox returns one indistinguishable error for all eight of its documented decline scenarios. The action selector and the outcome simulator also share one probability model, so the lift is a statement about that model rather than a measurement of the world. Both limits are disclosed in the decision log rather than fixed.

01 · Results

Baseline ladder vs. Recoup

The baseline runs one fixed ladder for every cause: notify, then retry at 24, 48 and 72 hours. Recoup branches on the cause first. Bands move retry success and conversion for both arms; the finding holds in all three.

The schedule ablation

The arms retried on different ladders and the sweep never varied that. So it was taken away rather than argued about: Recoup forced onto the baseline's own 24/48/72h, leaving channel choice as the only remaining difference. About 91% of the lift survives, in all four cohorts.
