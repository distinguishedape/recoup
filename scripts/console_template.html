      Which mechanism collected the money

      The pay-now link is credited more than it added. Credit goes to whichever mechanism collected the payment, so a customer it converts on day two is credited to it even when the day-four retry would have collected the same rupees. Against the identical run without it, the two eligible causes gained ₹1,87,405, not ₹3,25,341.

      Section 3 · Whether it holds3 bands × 4 cohorts

      Net recovery lift in every cell

      Recovery probabilities are assumptions, so every one is swept across a pessimistic, central and optimistic band, over four independent cohorts. A finding counts only if it holds in every cell — not on average.

      Target is +15% net recovery. Every cell clears it; the weakest is the one to judge the result by, not the strongest.
